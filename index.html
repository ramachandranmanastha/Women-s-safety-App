Machine Learning

Random Forest Safety Engine

Our ML model predicts a safety score (0–100) for each route using 10 real-world features.

⚠️
Crime Rate Index — 28% weight
Historical crime incidents per km² in the route area. Strongest predictor of danger.
📢
User Community Reports — 22% weight
Real reports from other women in the last 30 days near the route.
🚔
Police Station Proximity — 16% weight
Distance to nearest police station. Closer = safer score.
💡
Road Lighting + Time of Day — 20% weight
Night travel (8 PM–6 AM) with poor lighting significantly lowers safety score.

Safety Scores

What the score means

Every route gets a score from 0 to 100 with a clear colour-coded label.

80–100
✅ SAFE
Low crime area, police station nearby, well-lit road, no recent reports. Recommended for travel.
60–79
⚠️ MODERATE
Some risk factors present. Caution advised, especially at night. Not ideal but usable.
40–59
🟠 CAUTION
Notable safety concerns. Multiple risk factors. Consider alternate routes if available.
0–39
🔴 UNSAFE
High crime index, multiple recent reports, poor lighting. Strongly avoid this route.
